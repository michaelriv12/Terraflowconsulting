# Project memory

## Product intent & audience

- Website for an agricultural irrigation company that represents/officially distributes irrigation brands.
- Audience: agricultural clients, likely in Belize/Central America.
- Spanish-language site.

## Brand personality & voice

- Professional, corporate tone in Spanish.
- Emphasizes official representation and brand authority.

## Visual design & UX direction

- Section-based single-page layout; sections include: Divisiones, Marcas que Representamos, Metodología (in that order).
- Initial divisions strip (top of page) lists brand divisions with optional "Representación oficial en Belize" sublabel beneath selected brands (Rain Bird and IrrigEasy both show it).
- Division strip order: Riego & Drenaje, Rain Bird, IrrigEasy, Talento Humano.
- Brand cards use custom typographic cards with brand-specific colors and distinctive icons (not official logos).
- Hover effects on cards ("efecto sutil al pasar el cursor").
- Navigation: top nav menu, mobile menu, and footer links — all kept in sync when new sections are added.
- Multiple HTML files in the project (at least two pages share styling/structure); changes applied to both.
- Card grid redistributes after brand removal to avoid empty spaces.

## Decisions, constraints & rationale

- Represented brands (current): Rain Bird (official representation · Belize), IrrigEasy (official representation · Belize).
- Toro, Hunter, and Netafim were removed per user request; no longer referenced on any page.
- Pexels stock photo service is unavailable in this environment.
- Official brand logos not copied from external sites due to copyright; user must supply logo files (PNG/JPG/SVG) directly if they want them included.
- Brand cards currently use placeholder icons + brand colors as a safe alternative to official logos.

## Open questions & assumptions

- User may provide official brand logo files to replace the current icon-based cards.
- Whether the site should eventually include actual product imagery from each brand remains undecided.
- Exact company name and full scope of services not yet confirmed beyond irrigation.
